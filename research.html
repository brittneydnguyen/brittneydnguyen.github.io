Research Interests

I study science education and flower macroevolution! As a biology educator, it is imperative that I'm up to speed on both research in my craft and in my discipline. It is only through this balance that I can build a curriculum that lives and breathes as the students and content do.

Regarding science education, I'm interested in the impacts of large lecture teaching practices on student learning, affect, and persistence. Right now, I'm focused on the equity of group composition during two-stage exams.

As for flower macroevolution, I'm curious about the correlated evolution of different floral parts across different species as a response to transitions in pollinator type. An ulterior motive of my work is to inspire others to make use of our amazing herbariums, which are rich in both data and stories!
