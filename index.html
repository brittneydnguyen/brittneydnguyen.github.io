Bio Teaching Research Writing

Brittney D. Nguyễn is a lover.

I believe that love—when it is interpreted as the act of extending oneself to create a nurturing and dialogical environment—is the basis of anything and everything we deem meaningful. Love, then, is a praxis that guides our way of navigating the world. To love fully and truly requires you to investigate yourself, to identify what in the world you care about and why. After this search, you must apply yourself to bettering these causes.

As a fourth year Ph.D. candidate in the Evolution, Ecology, and Organismal Biology (EEOB) department at the University of California, Riverside, I seek to use love as a revolutionizing force for science education and evolution research. I interrogate how classroom practices change when you consider the whole student and their interactions with their peers, as well as how evolutionary conclusions change when you consider the traits of the whole organism and their interactions with other traits. Shoutout to my undergraduate research team for making my dissertation dreams come true!

For any and all things love, please feel free to contact me at brittdnguyen@gmail.com! If you, too, are afraid of your inbox, you might instead find me at the park walking my dogs, on stage doing improv, or at the library reading and writing with fervor.
